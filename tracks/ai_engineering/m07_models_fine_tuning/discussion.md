# Discussion questions: m07_models_fine_tuning

Questions for a discussion drill: answer out loud, at your level, with a concrete
example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

1. How do you choose a model for a task? Which numbers do you compare, and which do you measure yourself?
2. When would you choose a local or self-hosted model over a hosted API, and what do you give up?
3. What is quantisation, and what do you trade for the smaller memory?
4. Prompting, RAG or fine-tuning: how do you decide? What does each fix and not fix?
5. What goes into a good fine-tuning dataset, and how can it go wrong?
6. What is LoRA, and why does it need so much less memory?
7. A company wants one model for redaction and another for hard questions. How would you split the work?
8. How can synthetic data help, and how can it quietly harm a fine-tune or an evaluation?
