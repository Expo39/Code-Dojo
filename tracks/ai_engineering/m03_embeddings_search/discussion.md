# Discussion questions: m03_embeddings_search

Questions for a discussion drill: answer out loud, at your level, with a concrete
example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

1. What is an embedding, and what does it mean that two texts are close?
2. How do you choose chunk size and overlap, and what goes wrong at each extreme?
3. Keyword search or vector search for an error-code lookup? For "how do I get my money back"? Why?
4. What does a vector database do that a Python list and cosine similarity do not?
5. What does hybrid search add, and what does a reranker add on top?
6. Your documents change daily. How do you keep the index fresh without rebuilding everything?
7. How would you check that your embedding model suits your language and domain?
8. How do you ingest documents that change daily, including deletions, without rebuilding the whole index?
