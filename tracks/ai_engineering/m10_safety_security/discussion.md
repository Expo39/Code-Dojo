example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

2. What is excessive agency, and how do you limit it?
3. Which actions should never run without a human approving them?

5. A company has sensitive customer data. Which AI work can go to a hosted model, which needs a private deployment, and what would you ask the provider?
6. Why is a model refusing a request not a security control?
7. What does the EU AI Act ask of a team that ships a chatbot?
