# Discussion questions: m04_rag

Questions for a discussion drill: answer out loud, at your level, with a concrete
example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

1. Explain RAG to a non-technical person, then to an engineer.
2. A user gets a wrong answer although the right document is in the index. How do you find out why?
3. How do you make a RAG answer cite its sources, and what should it do when the sources do not answer the question?
4. With a million-token context window, do we still need RAG? Argue both sides.
5. How would you evaluate a RAG system end to end, and its retrieval step on its own?
6. What are the security risks of RAG (data access, injected instructions in documents)?
7. Which would you try first for a few hundred documents: RAG, long context or a keyword search tool? Why?
