# Discussion questions: m08_rest_apis

Questions for a discussion drill: answer out loud, at your level, with a concrete
example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

1. What makes an API RESTful, and where do real APIs bend the rules?
2. Serializer or form: what is the same and what differs?
3. ViewSet or APIView: how do you choose?
4. How do you authenticate a browser app, a mobile app and a machine client against the same API?
5. How do you add a field or remove one without breaking existing clients?
6. What does the OpenAPI schema give you beyond documentation?
7. What is the cost of letting clients filter and sort on any field?
