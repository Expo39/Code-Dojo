# Discussion questions: m09_project_structure

Questions for a discussion drill: answer out loud, at your level, with a concrete
example; the tutor follows up (why? when would you not?) and judges against the read-first sources (track
README). No answers here on purpose.

1. Where does business logic belong: models, views, serializers or services? What do you gain and lose with each?
2. When is a signal the right tool, and when does it become a hidden bug?
3. How do you organise settings for development, tests and production without leaking secrets?
4. How do you decide between a management command, a Celery task and a cron job?
5. How do feature flags help a release, and what is the cost of leaving them in?
6. What should an audit trail record, and how do you make it hard to tamper with?
7. How do you stop two apps importing each other?
